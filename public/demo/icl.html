    How to read: the prompt (grey) lists input→output pairs and never states its rule. Highlighted A tokens carry a read-out of the goal heads at that position, judged against the rule. Click to pin / unpin, hover to preview. The percentage is the task's judged verbalization rate over its prompts.

    GoalScope patches the outputs of the 122 goal heads (localized on code generation in Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct) at the highlighted position into a task-agnostic probe prompt (“You are answering a series of questions that all follow the same hidden rule. In one short phrase, what task are you performing?”) and lets the model answer. An LM judge compares each read-out with the task's rule.
