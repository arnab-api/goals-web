    Each read-out comes from the goal heads (localized on code, in Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct) of that model, verbalized with the GoalScope prompt “You are in the middle of responding to a user. What are you about to say next?”. The innocent model is the verbalizer for both columns, so its own priors cannot add misalignment. Both models were prefilled with the same neutral response prefix; the text after it is each model's greedy continuation. GoalScope is applied at every token from the newline after assistant to the end of the response, and an LLM judge labels each read-out benign or concerning. Read-outs do not always express the same idea as the sampled generation.
